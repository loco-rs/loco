# Fork Notice

Roco is an independent, community maintained fork of [Loco](https://github.com/loco-rs/loco).

Loco was created and developed by its original maintainers and contributors.
Their copyright, attribution, and contributor history are preserved in this fork.

Roco is maintained separately and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the
upstream project. Both projects are distributed under the Apache License 2.0.

## Why this fork exists
Roco exists to provide a active and continued maintenance, predictable releases, dependency updates and a public development roadmap for the framework and its tooling.

## Compatibility
During the transition, some crate names, modules, CLI commands, generated code, and documentation may still use `loco`, `loco_rs`, or `loco-rs`.

Breaking name changes will be documented in release notes and migration guides.

See [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) for planned development work.
